    Waking her

    Gull sleeps with one ear open. Say wakey wakey Gull, hey, I'm home or hey, I'm back and she opens her eye and greets you. You can tap the eye too. Add your question in the same breath (I'm back, what's the market doing?) and she answers it straight after the greeting.

    Talking to her

    Once she's awake she keeps listening; just speak. What she says appears as subtitles under the eye. You can talk over her: she stops mid-sentence and listens (a bare hold on or wait just stops her). Headphones make this work best. Try what's going on in the market, what's the top news and how does it affect us, should I trade right now, show me the 4 hour chart, log this trade, how's my journal, what would change your mind, or ask her anything about spot trading: order types, OCO, position sizing, RSI, sessions, CPI days.

    When she shows you something, the eye moves to the left and the panel opens on the right. Say close it or press Esc to bring her back full screen. Say goodnight Gull or that's all to put her to sleep; she also dozes off by herself after a few quiet minutes (Settings).

    Before any trade

    She refreshes the live data, makes sure the news scan is less than an hour old, then studies the past (daily trend, 7/30/90-day moves, where price sits in its 300-day range, structure), the present (4-hour momentum, 15-minute trigger, volume, the order book, Fear & Greed, which session it is) and what's likely next (bull, base and bear scenarios with odds, the expected 24-hour range, the events ahead). The Analysis panel shows the case with the exact entry, stop, two targets, reward/risk, position size, the dollars you lose at the stop, and how to place it on Binance as a buy plus an OCO.

    The rules she won't break

    1. No buy when reward/risk to the first target is under 1.5.
    2. No buy when the news desk flags high event risk inside its horizon.
    3. The position is sized so the stop loses your set risk percent, never more.
    4. Counter-trend setups are flagged: half size or skip.
    5. Spot only. "Bearish" means stay in USDT or sell what you hold.

    If she suggests a buy that breaks one of these, the page overrides her and shows WAIT, with the reason.

    Cost

    A normal question is a fraction of a cent to a few cents. A news scan adds about a cent per web search plus tokens: a few cents to 15 cents. A full trade analysis usually costs 2–10 cents, more if it has to scan the news first. Set a monthly limit in the Anthropic console.

    What she can't do

    See the future, place trades, or be right every time. A 55% win rate with 2:1 reward/risk is a good system and still loses 45 of 100 trades. Run the paper journal for 30 to 60 days before real money.

    Privacy

    Prices come from Binance's public endpoints straight from your browser. Fear & Greed comes from alternative.me. Her natural voice (Kokoro, open source) runs on this device; the model downloads once from Hugging Face and no audio is sent anywhere. The Anthropic key lives on your server; you reach it by signing in. Voice recognition uses your browser's speech service (Google's in Chrome) while the microphone is on; turn it off with the mic button. Journal, news scans and settings sync through your Supabase database.
